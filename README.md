Hospitalityservis
Where hospitality connects, evaluates, and grows.
The professional network connecting restaurant operators with trusted partners, technology, and services.
The business vision
Hospitalityservis brings together two sides of the industry:
1. Restaurant operators looking for reliable providers, honest reviews, comparisons, and better solutions for their businesses.
2. Vendors and service providers looking to build credibility, reach qualified restaurant customers, and grow their revenue.
The platform helps operators discover and evaluate providers, then connects them with the right businesses through qualified introductions and measurable commercial opportunities.
How the business makes money
 Reviews and reputation: Build trust through authentic reviews, provider profiles, ratings, and comparisons.
 Industry network and discovery: Attract restaurant operators and help them find relevant technology, financing, suppliers, and service partners.
 Qualified lead generation: Route interested operators to selected providers through demos, quote requests, consultations, and introductions.
 Revenue share and cost per lead: Monetize provider relationships through referral commissions, agreed revenue shares, and fees for qualified leads.

1. How the go-to-market model works
1. Call local restaurants and food trucks: Introduce Hospitalityservis and explain that you're gathering honest feedback from hospitality operators about their current business setup.
2. Learn how they operate: Ask about their POS, payment processing, food suppliers, phone systems, reservations, marketing, and other operating expenses.
3. Collect honest feedback: Invite them to share their experience for the review platform. Be clear about what the review involves and obtain permission before publishing any feedback.
4. Identify opportunities to save: Based on their needs, identify relevant local or industry providers that may offer better pricing, better service, or a more suitable solution.
5. Make a useful introduction: With the operator's permission, connect them with a suitable provider, arrange a quote or demo, or help them compare alternatives.
6. Track results and monetize: Follow up to see whether the business actually benefited. Earn agreed referral revenue or qualified-lead fees from participating providers.
2. The value proposition to restaurant owners
We don't want the initial call to sound like another salesperson trying to sell them something. We want it to sound like a useful industry conversation.
The message is:
 We want to hear directly from hospitality businesses about what's working and what isn't.
 We're building a review and resource platform to help businesses make more informed decisions.
 We help operators discover relevant local services and providers.
 Where appropriate, we can introduce them to alternatives that may reduce operating costs or improve their setup.
 The operator decides whether to explore any recommendation; there is no obligation to switch providers.
The savings message should be grounded in real examples and substantiated results. If we say other businesses have saved money, we should be able to explain the type of service, the basis of the comparison, and the circumstances behind those savings.

4. What we should ask during the call
The questions help us understand the operator's needs and identify relevant providers.
 Payments and POS: Are you happy with your current POS and payment processing setup, including the fees you're paying?
 Suppliers and purchasing: Are your current food suppliers meeting your needs on pricing, consistency, and delivery?
 Phone and ordering: How do you currently handle incoming calls, missed orders, reservations, and catering inquiries?
 Marketing and customer growth: Are you getting the results you want from your marketing and customer-retention efforts?
 Biggest business challenge: If you could improve one part of your operation or reduce one expense, what would it be?
We should not assume every restaurant needs a new provider. Sometimes the right outcome is confirming that the operator already has a good solution. That honesty is what makes the review platform credible.

A critical safeguard: a positive review must never be conditional on accepting an introduction, purchasing a service, or giving us a referral. Reviews should reflect genuine experiences, with clear disclosure of any commercial relationships and appropriate consent for publishing feedback or sharing contact details.
6. The local advantage
The initial strategy should be geographically focused. Build relationships with restaurants and food trucks in a defined market, understand their most common challenges, recruit suitable providers, and develop a reliable track record of useful introductions.
As the platform grows, Hospitalityservis can build a local network of:
 POS and payment providers.
 Food suppliers and distributors.
 Restaurant financing providers.
 AI phone, ordering, and reservation solutions.
 Marketing and customer-retention services.
 Equipment, maintenance, and operational support.
Over time, the review content attracts more operators, operator conversations reveal new needs, and the provider network creates more opportunities to help those businesses.
